From our [verification method](/about/): **Verified** means the repository is reachable, its links respond, and (for repos) it had a commit in the last 180 days. **Under review** means a listing is not currently counted as Verified; we re-check every listing weekly. **Not currently verified** covers listings with no commit in more than 180 days, and repositories archived by their owners. Under review is not a statement that a tool is down or unsafe.
